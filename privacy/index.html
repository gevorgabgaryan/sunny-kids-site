Information for grown-ups

Privacy policy

Sunny Kids keeps learning on your device. The app does not send children’s learning information to Sunny Academy or another service.

Effective November 17, 2026 · Applies to Sunny Kids version 0.1.0

Who provides Sunny Kids

Sunny Kids is provided by Sunny Academy, operated by SUNNY, LLC (Armenia). This policy covers the Sunny Kids offline English preview app. Other Sunny Academy websites and services have their own policies.

Privacy questions: support@sunnyacademy.ai.

Information saved on your device

Build 7 and later save up to six sibling nicknames, learning and instruction-language settings, daily time limits, sound and recording preferences, explored words, favourite words in build 9 and later, practice evidence, completed activities, drawings, story choices and daily learning time locally. Earlier builds save one local nickname and learning profile. A nickname is enough; a child’s full name is not needed.

Sunny Kids does not transmit this information to us or another service and does not sync progress between devices. We cannot view a child’s local profile or restore its progress remotely.

No child accounts or tracking

Build 7 and earlier have no parent accounts. Sunny Kids has no child accounts, ads, purchases, chat, location access or camera access. It does not request a child’s email, photo or date of birth. Build 6 and earlier have no microphone recording.

Pictures, words, stories and English lesson audio are included in the app. Lesson playback is offline and does not send text or recordings to a speech service.

Optional speaking practice in build 7 and later

Recording is off by default. A grown-up can enable it and grant microphone permission in parent settings. A child may then record one practice clip of up to 15 seconds and replay or delete it. The temporary recording is stored only in the device cache and is never uploaded, shared or scored.

Recordings are deleted when changing the practice word, when the activity closes or the app enters the background. If the app stops unexpectedly, an unfinished temporary recording is removed on the next launch when the cache directory is available. Temporary recordings are not intentionally included in app backups.

Parent accounts in build 8 and later

A parent can sign in with a Sunny Academy account or create one using their own first name, email and password. Registration sends a verification email. Sign-in and account recovery need an internet connection. The app sends these parent account details securely to Sunny Academy and stores session tokens in protected device storage; it does not save the password. These account services follow Sunny Academy’s privacy policy and Terms.

Children use local profiles without an email or password. Parent sign-in does not upload or sync their learning progress, drawings or recordings. Parents can sign out on this phone. In build 10 and later, Delete parent account in the parent area offers direct account deletion with a confirmation. Earlier builds open the account deletion website. Deleting the shared account closes access to other Sunny Academy services, normally erases its personal data within 48 hours and keeps legally required records. Local child profiles and artwork remain until a grown-up deletes them separately. Existing account information may also be used by other Sunny Academy services under their published privacy policy.

Parent account security and emails

Sunny Academy receives the parent account identifier, IP address, approximate location inferred from that IP, and account actions such as sign-in or registration. These support account access, fraud prevention and security monitoring. Its email and security service providers process the information needed for those services. New Sunny Academy accounts receive daily learning emails; a parent can unsubscribe using the link in an email or their Sunny Academy email preferences. Account names and email addresses are used for these parent communications. This processing applies to the optional adult account, not children’s offline play, and does not track families across other companies’ apps for advertising.

Optional device unlock

A grown-up can enable an optional device-unlock check for parent settings. iOS handles Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode and returns whether the check succeeded. Sunny Kids does not receive or store biometric information or the passcode.

The grown-up multiplication challenge remains available. These checks help separate settings from play; they do not establish identity, age or verified parental consent.

Keeping and clearing local information

A grown-up can select “Clear local learning progress” in the parent area. This clears that explorer’s learning and drawings while keeping the nickname and settings. In build 7 and later, a grown-up can also clear a gallery or delete a sibling profile and all its local information. The app’s recovery option can remove saved settings and progress if local data cannot be opened.

Uninstalling the app can remove its local information. Device backups may include app information depending on your device’s backup settings. Sunny Kids does not control Apple’s or other device backups.

When a grown-up contacts support

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to answer the request and resolve the issue. Our email provider processes the message to deliver and store it. We do not sell support correspondence or use it to advertise to children.

Please do not send a child’s full name, photo, voice, password or other private information. Device model, iOS version, app version and a description of the problem are usually enough. A grown-up can email us to request deletion of support correspondence; we will handle the request subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

This help website

These pages do not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or forms. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement. This website processing is separate from the offline app.

Future changes and questions

If a future app version changes these practices, we will update this policy and its effective date. No subscription or future family plan is activated in version 0.1.0.

A parent or guardian can contact support@sunnyacademy.ai with questions about this policy. Include “Sunny Kids privacy” in the subject.
