Privacy policy

Guest learning stays on your device. Child sign-in and separately approved backup use Sunny Academy’s online services.

Effective November 23, 2026 · Current Sunny Kids 0.1.0 preview

Who provides Sunny Kids

Sunny Kids is provided by Sunny Academy, operated by SUNNY, LLC (Armenia). This policy covers the Kids preview app. Other Sunny Academy services have their own policies. Questions: support@sunnyacademy.ai.

Local learning and profiles

The device stores up to six local child profiles, nicknames, settings, practiced and favourite words, learning evidence, completed activities and adventure steps, drawings, story choices and learning time. Guest learning requires no email, full name, date of birth or photo. Included activities and audio work offline.

Optional child accounts

A parent or legal guardian creates a child account in Sunny Academy and approves storage of the child’s first name, last name and unique username. Kids asks only for the child’s username and password. Credentials are sent securely to Sunny Academy; the server stores a salted password hash, account identity, guardian approval, sessions and credit balance. Online services receive the technical request information, including IP address, needed to handle requests. A child session cannot access adult account management or purchases.

The app saves session credentials and cached account identity in protected device storage, but does not save the password. Access credentials last one minute; rotating renewal credentials expire seven days after sign-in. The backend checks account status for protected requests. Sign-in alone does not upload learning. Kids has no adult registration, Apple sign-in or Google sign-in.

Optional progress backup

A parent must separately approve learning backup in Sunny Academy. Sync in Kids’ protected parent area can upload practiced words, learning evidence, activity completion, daily adventure steps and learning time. Nicknames, parent settings, drawings and recordings are excluded. Each installation contributes its own progress; retries do not count twice, completed steps are preserved and changes from separate installations are combined. Turning backup off removes the online copy. Existing offline learning remains on each device.

Account recovery and deletion

Parents reset child passwords, assign existing credits, return unused credits and delete child accounts in the Sunny Academy child accounts section. Password reset revokes existing child sessions. Deleting the child account closes online access and returns unused credits. Closing the parent account closes child online access and starts personal-data erasure, normally within 48 hours. Financial records may be retained where legally required. Restricted operational backups retain archived data for up to 30 days.

For parent account deletion or a request without signing in, use Sunny Academy’s account-deletion page. Online account deletion does not erase learning already stored on a device. Clear local learning or delete the local profile separately in the protected parent area. Sign-out clears local session credentials even offline; server sign-out is attempted when connected.

Microphone and recordings

Record-and-replay practice is off by default. A grown-up must enable it and grant microphone permission. Practice clips last at most 15 seconds, remain temporarily on the device, and are never uploaded or scored. They are deleted when changing the word, leaving the activity or backgrounding the app. Abandoned practice files are removed on startup when the cache is accessible. Listening and speaking without recording remain available when permission is denied.

Online content and unavailable features

Optional catalog checks and media downloads contact the content service. Published remote media is checked before use and downloads can be removed in the app. Included media remains available offline. The preview has no advertising, tracking analytics, purchases or subscriptions.

Ask Sunny is off by default. Before any future activation, it requires authenticated parental approval for text processing, separate optional external transcription consent, spending limits and verified provider, language and platform settings. The local parent gate does not grant this consent. Failed or blocked questions are not charged; answer recovery lasts 24 hours and optional history lasts 30 days, with parent deletion. Provider processing and retention must be disclosed before consent. Scan & Learn, camera/gallery scanning and cloud voice correction remain disabled. Previously saved local scan text or generated activities remain until cleared; adults should review AI-generated activities because AI can make mistakes.

Parent gate and clearing local information

The grown-up challenge protects local settings and limits. Optional device unlock is handled by iOS or Android; Sunny Kids receives no biometric information or device passcode. A child who knows the device passcode may pass the device check, so the grown-up challenge is still required. These checks do not establish adult identity or verified parental consent.

Parents can clear learning and drawings or delete a local sibling profile. Clearing learning keeps nickname and settings. Uninstalling can remove local data; device backups may include it according to device settings. Sunny Kids does not control operating-system backups.

Support and this website

A grown-up who contacts support shares their email and message, which we use to respond and resolve problems. Do not send children’s names, photos, voice, passwords or device passcodes. Request deletion of support correspondence by emailing support, subject to applicable legal retention.

These help pages add no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or forms. GitHub Pages may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. We will update this policy when processing practices change.
