Information for grown-ups

How can we help?

Find answers about Sunny Kids, or contact us for help with your child’s English adventures.

Getting started in build 15

On the welcome screen, Parent sign in opens the login form and Create parent account opens registration directly. Start learning opens the child’s local setup without an account. Choose a nickname, starting level and daily time, then start the included offline activities. These welcome buttons no longer open the multiplication screen.

The grown-up challenge still protects parent settings after learning has started. If you see the calculation when using the welcome buttons, install build 15 or later from TestFlight.

Contact Sunny Kids support

Email support@sunnyacademy.ai with “Sunny Kids” in the subject.

Please tell us the device model, iOS version, Sunny Kids version and what happened. If a screenshot helps, remove names and other private information first.

A parent or guardian should contact us. Please do not send a child’s full name, photo, voice recording, password or device passcode.

I cannot hear words or stories

  1. Open the grown-up area and turn on “Spoken words and stories.” Save the setting.
  2. Tap Listen and raise the device’s media volume. Check whether sound is being sent to Bluetooth headphones, a speaker or AirPlay.
  3. Build 6 and later include English lesson audio inside the app. No English device voice download is needed.
  4. Try Listen again. If a retry message appears, tap it. If you are using TestFlight, install the latest available Sunny Kids build.

An explicit Listen action can play while the iPhone Ring/Silent switch is on Silent. Volume, sound routing and the app’s sound setting still affect playback.

Finding and saving words in build 9

Open My words or Explore, then tap Find a word. Search by an English word or its meaning in the selected Armenian or Russian language. Choose a topic or use My words, Favourites or Practise again to narrow the library.

Tap the star beside a word to save it for the selected child. Tap it again to remove it. Favourites stay on this device, do not count as mastered words, and remain separate for siblings. English narration remains English.

What works offline?

The included pictures, words, stories and activities work without signing in. Build 6 and later include offline audio for every word, story and activity instruction. Downloading the app or updates requires an internet connection.

Build 7 expands the original 16-word library to 65 English words, with drawing and a saved gallery, logic, math, discovery, stories, early reading, nature, music, short offline learning films and coding. Starter and Growing guide difficulty; they are not assessed proficiency levels. Earlier builds include the four original word worlds.

How do I open grown-up settings?

Open the grown-up area and complete the multiplication challenge. If optional device unlock is enabled, you can use it before the challenge or choose the grown-up check instead.

Anyone enrolled on the device, or someone who knows its passcode, may complete the device-unlock check. It does not verify that a person is a parent.

Why has learning stopped for today?

A grown-up chooses a daily learning limit from 5 to 30 minutes. Time counts while the child area is open. Background time and the grown-up settings area do not count.

A new day starts at midnight according to the device’s clock. A grown-up can change the limit in settings and save it.

Where is learning progress saved?

Build 7 and later keep up to six separate sibling profiles on the device. Select an explorer in the parent area; each has separate progress, settings, drawings and daily time. Earlier builds have one profile. Progress does not sync to another iPhone or iPad, and support cannot retrieve it from a server.

“Clear local learning progress” resets learning while keeping the nickname and settings. Use it only when you want to reset that progress. Uninstalling the app can remove local information; device backups depend on your device settings.

If saving fails, try saving settings again. If saved learning cannot be opened, try the recovery screen’s “Try again” first. “Start fresh” removes saved settings and progress on that device and cannot be undone.

Welcome, language and parent sign-in

Build 8 starts with a visible English, Armenian or Russian language choice. A grown-up can sign in or create a Sunny Academy account, then set up the child’s nickname, learning level and daily time. Children use their local profiles. Offline setup remains available.

The language button on the home screen changes setup, home navigation and activity guides; English learning words and narration stay in English. Some activity labels remain English. Parent account sign-in, registration and password recovery require an internet connection. Account sign-in does not sync children’s progress between phones.

Registration requires a parent email verification code. If you forget your password, request a reset link, open it from your email, then return to sign in. Build 10 and later offer direct deletion of the shared Sunny Academy parent account from the parent account section, with confirmation. Earlier builds open the deletion website. This closes access across Sunny Academy; children’s local profiles remain until a grown-up deletes them separately. “From the Developer” is Apple’s TestFlight introduction; tap Continue to open Sunny Kids.

Delete a Sunny Kids parent account

Sunny Kids uses an optional shared Sunny Academy account. To request deletion without the Kids app, open Sunny Academy account deletion and follow the account confirmation steps there. You can also open Parent area → Parent account → Delete parent account in Sunny Kids, confirm the account email and type DELETE.

Deletion closes the shared account immediately across Sunny Kids, Sunny Academy and its website. The account profile, sign-in details and server learning data are normally permanently erased within 48 hours. Legally required payment records may be retained. Cancel any existing Sunny Plus subscription before deletion; deletion does not refund purchases or credits.

Children’s nicknames, learning progress and drawings are stored only on the device and are not part of the shared account. Clear learning or delete a local profile separately in Parent area. If you cannot access your account, contact support@sunnyacademy.ai from its registered email for deletion help.

Which children can use Sunny Kids?

The main App Store Kids age group is 6–8. Children aged 9–11 can also explore the activities with a grown-up. Choose the sentence length and daily limit that suit your child, and read together.

This first version is a small English practice library. It has no child accounts, ads, purchases or subscriptions.

Instructions, learning evidence and speaking

In build 7 and later, parents can choose English, Armenian or Russian activity guides. Spoken learning models and replayable instructions remain English; build 8 also translates setup and home navigation. Some activity labels remain English.

The parent summary distinguishes practice, answers with help, independent answers and recall on separate days. Activity completion is participation, not a claim of mastery. A short family activity is suggested for practice away from the screen.

Optional record-and-replay is off by default. Enable it and grant microphone access in parent settings, then save. If access is denied, listening and speaking without a recording still work. Clips stay temporarily on the device and are deleted when the activity closes or the app backgrounds. Nothing is uploaded or scored.

Privacy and licenses

Read the Sunny Kids privacy policy for details about local information and optional device unlock. The grown-up area inside the app includes the privacy policy and illustration, voice and software licenses.

Scan & Learn in build 14

Open Explore → Scan & Learn. Choose Camera or an image, crop or rotate, then correct recognised English text. Select paragraphs, listen, pause, replay or change speed. Save and reopen pages in the child’s offline library. Practise uses a missing-word exercise; opening an answer does not count as successful recall. Camera denial and missing voices allow typing and reading together. Online translation and explanations are unavailable in this beta.
