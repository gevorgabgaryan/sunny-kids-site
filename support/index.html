Information for grown-ups

How can we help?

Help your child start, return confidently and stop comfortably.

Start and sign in

Choose guest learning and create a local nickname, or use a child username and password created by a parent in Sunny Academy. Kids does not create adult accounts and has no Apple or Google sign-in. Use the Sunny Academy app or website to create or manage child accounts.

Finish a daily adventure

Choose Start today’s adventure. Read or listen to the story, play the picture game, optionally listen and repeat, then revise words. Hints and replay are available. Leave whenever your child needs a break: completed steps stay saved; an unfinished step restarts on return. Completion ends with a natural stopping point.

I cannot hear words or stories

  1. Check sound in the protected parent settings and the device’s media volume.
  2. Replay the instruction or word. Included lesson audio works offline.
  3. Some device read-aloud needs an installed English voice. Download the voice in device settings while connected.
  4. Children can read captions or pictures with a grown-up and continue without sound.

Profiles, limits and progress

Use the grown-up challenge to open parent settings. Manage separate local profiles, daily learning time, viewing limits, the starting level and optional recording. Videos also count toward learning time. Viewing can be disabled and continuous autoplay is off.

The summary shows completed activities, practiced words, time and skills needing revision, plus an activity to do together. Completion is participation, not proof of mastery.

Backup and changing devices

Learning saves offline separately for each child. Parents can approve optional backup in Sunny Academy, then use Sync learning in Kids’ protected parent area. Sign-in alone does not sync. Only words, learning evidence, completed steps and time are backed up; drawings, recordings and settings remain local. Repeated sync does not duplicate progress; separate installation contributions are combined. Do not uninstall before syncing anything you wish to keep.

Password recovery, credits and deletion

Open Manage child accounts in the protected parent area, or sign in to Sunny Academy and open the Sunny Kids accounts section. A parent can reset the child password, allocate existing credits, return unused credits or delete the child account. Children cannot manage the parent account or make purchases in Kids. Zero credits does not block free offline learning.

Password reset closes existing child sessions. Account deletion closes online access; clear local progress or delete the local profile separately on every device. For parent account deletion or a request without signing in, use Sunny Academy’s account-deletion page.

Recording permission

Record-and-replay is optional and off by default. A parent enables it and grants microphone access in settings. If permission is denied, listen and repeat without recording. Practice clips are temporary, last up to 15 seconds, are never uploaded or scored, and are deleted on leaving or backgrounding.

Availability and privacy

This help describes the current local preview, primarily for ages 6–8. The main child and parent guidance is available in 36 languages. Translations are machine-assisted, and learning content and some activity labels still need language review. The full policy is available in English. No ads or purchases are enabled. Ask Sunny and Scan & Learn remain disabled. The larger cartoon collection still needs approved finished media.

Store and TestFlight versions may differ from this preview. Read the privacy policy and the licenses in the protected parent area.

Contact support

A parent or guardian can email support@sunnyacademy.ai. Include device model, operating-system version, app version and what happened. Remove private information from screenshots. Never send a child’s full name, photo, voice, password or device passcode.
