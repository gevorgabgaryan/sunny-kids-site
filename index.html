Sunny Kids is an offline English practice app with pictures, spoken words, short stories and matching activities.

A gentle first library

Version 0.1.0 includes 16 English words and four short stories across Forest, Garden, Pond and Home. Four activity types invite children to explore words, listen, follow a story and match pictures.

Starter and Growing settings change sentence length. This is a small practice library to enjoy together, rather than an assessed English course.

The main App Store Kids age group is 6–8. Children aged 9–11 can also use the activities with a grown-up’s guidance.
