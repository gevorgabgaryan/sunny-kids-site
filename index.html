Sunny Kids brings English, thinking and creativity together through calm offline activities, with learning progress parents can understand.

A welcoming start

Build 8 adds a clear language chooser, parent sign-in and registration, and guided child setup. Parents can use their Sunny Academy account; children learn through local profiles. Account sign-in does not sync child learning between devices.

A gentle first library

Build 7 expands the original word worlds into a 65-word English library with daily discovery trails, creative drawing and a saved gallery, logic, math, nature, stories, music, early reading and coding. Short offline learning films lead to a follow-up activity.

Starter and Growing guide difficulty. Parents can choose English, Armenian or Russian activity guides and manage separate sibling profiles. The app offers practice and creation; its progress summary is not a formal assessment.

The main App Store Kids age group is 6–8. Children aged 9–11 can also use the activities with a grown-up’s guidance.
