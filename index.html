For young explorers and their grown-ups

Small English adventures.
Time to explore together.

Stories, picture games, optional speaking and word revision, with a comfortable place to stop.

Start today’s adventure

Complete a short story, play a picture game, optionally listen and repeat, then revisit words. Completed steps stay saved for each child, and unfinished adventures can be resumed. Included lessons, games and audio work offline.

Learning at your child’s pace

A starting assessment helps recommend practice. Gentle hints, replay and revision support words that need another try. Explore the word library, drawing, maths, early reading and coding. Progress shows practice and learning evidence, rather than promising mastery.

Accounts managed by grown-ups

Guest learning needs only a local nickname. Parents create child usernames and passwords in the Sunny Academy app or website; Sunny Kids supports child sign-in only. Parents manage account recovery, existing credits and account deletion in Sunny Academy.

Local parent settings protect profile switching, daily learning and viewing limits, optional recording and optional parent-approved progress backup.

Watch & Learn

Original short Sunny animations include captions, replay, speed controls and related practice. Continuous autoplay is off. Only published media is shown; the larger episode collection is still being prepared.

Privacy and availability

Learning stays local by default. Optional backup requires separate parent approval. There are no ads or purchases in this preview. Ask Sunny and Scan & Learn remain unavailable until their release checks and approvals are complete.

This page describes the current local preview; it does not confirm availability of this version in TestFlight, the App Store or Google Play.
